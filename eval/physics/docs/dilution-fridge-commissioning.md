# Commissioning report CR-DR2: dilution refrigerator with 12 T magnet, laboratory B214

*Made-up report for the Kovrune physics suite. The laboratory, the refrigerator and the measurements are invented.*

| | |
| --- | --- |
| System | Cryogen-free dilution refrigerator DR2, pulse-tube cooled, with a 12 T solenoid |
| Location | Laboratory B214, Institute for Quantum Materials |
| Commissioning period | 3 to 21 March 2026 |
| Prepared by | R. Osei (cryogenics group) |

## 1. Summary

The refrigerator meets all acceptance criteria of the purchase specification. The mixing chamber reached a base temperature of 8.2 mK, below the specified 10 mK. The cooling power at 100 mK is 450 μW, above the specified 400 μW. The magnet reached its rated field of 12.0 T on the third training ramp, after two quenches.

## 2. Cooldown

The first cooldown from room temperature took 36 h to base temperature with the experimental insert mounted. The temperatures of the stages at base temperature were:

| Stage | Temperature |
| --- | --- |
| 50 K plate (pulse tube, first stage) | 42 K |
| 4 K plate (pulse tube, second stage) | 3.1 K |
| Still | 0.80 K |
| Cold plate | 95 mK |
| Mixing chamber | 8.2 mK |

The mixing chamber temperature was measured with a calibrated ruthenium oxide (RuOx) thermometer and checked against a noise thermometer, which agreed within 0.3 mK at base temperature.

## 3. Cooling power

The cooling power of the mixing chamber was measured by applying heat with a resistive heater and recording the equilibrium temperature, with the still heater at its optimal setting of 4 mW.

| Mixing chamber temperature | Cooling power |
| --- | --- |
| 20 mK | 12 μW |
| 50 mK | 110 μW |
| 100 mK | 450 μW |
| 200 mK | 1.1 mW |

The specification requires at least 400 μW at 100 mK; the measured 450 μW passes with a margin of 50 μW. Without any heater power the parasitic heat load on the mixing chamber, estimated from the cooling power curve, is about 25 nW.

## 4. Magnet

The superconducting solenoid is rated for 12 T at a magnet temperature of 4.2 K. It was trained at a ramp rate of 0.2 T/min up to 10 T and 0.1 T/min above 10 T.

- First ramp: quench at 10.4 T.
- Second ramp: quench at 11.2 T.
- Third ramp: 12.0 T reached and held for 2 h without a quench.

After each quench the mixing chamber warmed to about 1.5 K and recovered to base temperature within 6 h. The field homogeneity is 0.1% over a 10 mm diameter spherical volume at the field centre. A compensation coil keeps the stray field at the mixing chamber below 5 mT when the magnet is at 12 T.

## 5. Vibrations and noise

The vibration level at the sample stage, measured with a geophone at room temperature with the pulse tube running, is 0.8 μm/s (RMS, 1 Hz to 1 kHz). The electron temperature of a test device on the sample stage, measured through Coulomb blockade thermometry, is 21 mK at a mixing chamber temperature of 8.2 mK; the difference comes from heating through the measurement wiring and will be reduced by additional filtering.

## 6. Acceptance

| Criterion | Specified | Measured | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base temperature | below 10 mK | 8.2 mK | Pass |
| Cooling power at 100 mK | at least 400 μW | 450 μW | Pass |
| Rated magnet field | 12 T | 12.0 T | Pass |
| Cooldown time | below 48 h | 36 h | Pass |

The system is accepted for use.
