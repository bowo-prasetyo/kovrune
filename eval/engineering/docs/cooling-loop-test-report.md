# Test report TR-210-03: Liquid cooling loop qualification

**Unit under test:** PSU-3K3 cooling loop, serial CL-0007, revision C hardware
**Specification:** CS-210 revision C · **Test dates:** 9–13 June 2026

## Summary

The cooling loop was tested against specification CS-210. It passed all thermal, hydraulic, environmental and leak tests. The heat exchanger was retrofitted with a turbulator insert after the first thermal run; the results below are for the retrofitted unit unless stated.

## Thermal test at rated load

The loop was run at the rated heat load of 4.0 kW with a 30% propylene glycol coolant until temperatures were stable for 30 minutes.

| Quantity | Measured | Requirement (CS-210) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold plate inlet temperature | 38.2 °C | max 45 °C |
| Cold plate outlet temperature | 52.7 °C | max 60 °C |
| Flow rate | 4.6 L/min | min 4.0 L/min |
| Loop pressure drop | 31 kPa | max 35 kPa |
| Heat exchanger effectiveness | 0.82 | min 0.75 |

With the turbulator insert, the heat exchanger effectiveness improved from 0.74 to 0.82. Before the retrofit, the first thermal run did not meet the effectiveness requirement.

## Environmental tests

After a cold soak of 12 hours at −20 °C, the pump started and reached 3200 rpm within 4 s. The loop then ran for 2 hours at an ambient temperature of 50 °C with no alarms.

## Leak test

The filled loop was pressurised to 3 bar for 10 minutes. The pressure loss was 0.2 kPa, within the 0.5 kPa allowed.

## Conclusion

The cooling loop with the turbulator insert meets every requirement of CS-210 revision C that was tested.
