SPECIFICATION CS-210
Liquid cooling loop for the PSU-3K3 power supply
Revision C

1. Scope

This specification sets the requirements for the liquid cooling loop that removes heat from the power stage of the PSU-3K3 power supply. It covers the pump, the cold plate, the heat exchanger and the hoses. Requirements use "shall". Verification is by test unless stated otherwise.

2. Thermal requirements

2.1 The coolant temperature at the cold plate inlet shall not exceed 45 °C at the rated heat load.
2.2 The coolant temperature at the cold plate outlet shall not exceed 60 °C at the rated heat load.
2.3 The loop shall remove a heat load of at least 3.5 kW continuously.
2.4 The heat exchanger effectiveness shall be at least 0.75 at the nominal flow rate.

3. Hydraulic requirements

3.1 The coolant flow rate shall be at least 4.0 L/min.
3.2 The pressure drop across the complete loop shall not exceed 35 kPa at the nominal flow rate.
3.3 The pump shall run at a nominal speed of 3200 rpm.
3.4 The bore of each hose fitting shall be 12.00 ± 0.05 mm.

4. Environmental requirements

4.1 The loop shall operate at ambient temperatures from −20 °C to 50 °C.
4.2 The loop shall withstand storage at temperatures from −40 °C to 85 °C, drained.
4.3 After a cold soak at −20 °C, the pump shall start and reach its nominal speed within 10 s.

5. Mechanical requirements

5.1 The pump mounting bracket shall have a safety factor of at least 1.5 against yield under the transport shock load case.
5.2 The first natural frequency of the pump mounting bracket shall be at least 120 Hz.
5.3 The deflection of the pump flange under the transport shock load case shall not exceed 1.0 mm.

6. Leak tightness

6.1 The filled loop shall be pressurised to 3 bar for 10 minutes. The pressure loss shall not exceed 0.5 kPa.

7. Coolant

7.1 The coolant shall be a 30% propylene glycol and water mixture.
