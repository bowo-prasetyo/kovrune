Kovrune is now at kovrune.pages.dev. Taking you there…

Your browser keeps saved data separately for each site, so the new address can't see it. To take it with you:

  1. Download a backup of it here.
  2. Open the new address, and set up the model again.
  3. Under Storage and backup, at the bottom of the research screen, choose Restore from a backup.

Downloaded models can't be moved: the new address downloads them again. The data here stays until you clear this site's data in the browser.
